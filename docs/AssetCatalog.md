## First Playable Minimum

The first runnable proof must include at least:

- 1 open map background
- 4 landmarks
- 1 player character
- 2 workstation/display assets
- 6 artifacts with damaged and repaired states
- 1 fixed UI panel with 2 action buttons
- 1 selection feedback behavior

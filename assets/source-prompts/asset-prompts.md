Use these prompts after the style direction is accepted. Keep outputs text-free and logo-free.

Include a restoration bureau area, stele rubbing courtyard, night-market commission stall, old relic sorting yard, warm stone paths, clay-red walls, aged-paper color palette, soft Guanzhong folk hand-painted texture, no strong horizon, no text, no labels, game background, 3072x1792.

Bottom-center pivot friendly, warm wood, clay red, aged paper, ink accents, readable at small scale, no text, no logos, each object centered with transparent padding.
